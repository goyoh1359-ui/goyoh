Privacy Policy & Data Compliance

Last updated: November 2026

At GOYOH Health Connect Private Limited (located in BTM Layout, Bengaluru, Karnataka), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting client data security in accordance with Indian data protection standards, IT Act guidelines, and Meta API policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect administrative details, operator credentials, and contact numbers strictly required for sending transactional messaging notifications, queue updates, and digital documents in 10 seconds.

2. Meta & Cloud API Data Usage

In compliance with Meta platform requirements, data shared via Cloud API webhooks is used solely for message delivery, reminders, and queue tracking. We do not use Meta user data for unauthorized cross-app tracking or advertising.

3. Data Security & Transmission

All data transmitted through our webhooks and cloud servers is encrypted in transit using secure SSL protocols. We do not sell, rent, or share records or phone numbers with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Deletion Requests

Users and enterprise partners can request complete removal of their account data and associated records at any time. To request data purging, please email us directly at goyoh571@gmail.com with your registered phone number or ID. Upon verification, all records will be permanently purged from our active databases.
